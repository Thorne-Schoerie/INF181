Data & Information Management

Managing student, academic, and financial data to support better decision-making in higher education.

Understanding Data and Information

Data

Data consists of raw facts such as student numbers, marks, attendance records, and fee balances.

Information

Information is processed data that helps management make informed decisions.

Information Quality

✅

Accuracy

Information should be correct and free from errors.

✅

Completeness

All required information should be captured.

✅

Consistency

Information should be the same across all systems.

✅

Timeliness

Information should be updated regularly.
