Enterprise Systems & Integration

Connecting Student Management, Learning Management and Finance Systems to improve data quality and decision-making.

What are Enterprise Systems?

Enterprise systems are large software systems used to manage information and business processes across an organisation. In higher education, they support student administration, learning, and financial management.

When these systems work together, institutions can access accurate information and provide better services to students (Laudon & Laudon, 2024).

Core Institutional Systems

Student Management System

Stores student registrations, academic records and programme information.

Learning Management System

Stores assignments, assessments, attendance records and learning materials.

Finance System

Stores student fee information, payments and financial aid records.

The Current Challenge

Audit Finding

The Student Management System, Learning Management System, and Finance System operate independently. This results in duplicated information, inconsistent records and reporting delays.
