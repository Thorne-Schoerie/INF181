Artificial Intelligence & Decision Support Systems

Using data, analytics and intelligent systems to improve decision-making and student success.

What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to computer systems that can analyse large amounts of data, identify patterns, and support decision-making.

In higher education, AI can help institutions improve student success, manage resources effectively and identify potential risks before they become serious problems (Laudon & Laudon, 2024).

Decision Support Systems (DSS)

A Decision Support System (DSS) helps managers and administrators make informed decisions using data, reports and analytical tools.

Rather than relying on guesswork, management can use DSS information to plan interventions and improve institutional performance.

AI Applications in Higher Education

At-Risk Student Detection

AI can identify students who may be struggling based on attendance and assessment results.

Student Chatbots

AI chatbots can answer common questions about registration, timetables and fees.

Predictive Analytics

AI can identify trends and predict future student performance.
