Adapting Information Systems

Customising information systems to improve student support, reporting and institutional efficiency.

What is System Customisation?

System customisation refers to modifying or configuring information systems to meet the specific requirements of an organisation.

Higher education institutions have unique requirements, which means systems should support their academic, administrative and financial processes.
